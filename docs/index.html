Abstract

We present a real-time system for detecting fingertip contact events on flat surfaces using monocular depth estimation and motion analysis. Our approach fine-tunes Depth Anything V2 on a custom close-range dataset captured with an Intel RealSense D405 depth camera, reducing mean absolute depth error from 12.8mm to 3.2mm in the 25-45cm operating range. Contact detection employs a velocity-gated hysteresis state machine that fuses depth-based surface distance (4.5mm entry / 6.0mm exit thresholds) with fingertip motion velocity cues, achieving 94.2% accuracy and 94.4% F1-score. The system operates at 30 FPS on consumer hardware, enabling practical vision-based virtual keyboard interaction at 45.6 words per minute with 3.1% character error rate. We release a multi-user, multi-angle D405 depth dataset comprising 53,300 RGB-depth pairs from 15 participants at four camera angles, with per-frame hand landmark annotations and contact/hover labels.

Method

System Pipeline. An RGB camera captures the typing scene. Our fine-tuned Depth Anything V2 estimates metric depth, from which fingertip-to-surface distances are computed. A velocity-gated hysteresis state machine fuses depth distance and fingertip motion to detect contact events in real-time.
